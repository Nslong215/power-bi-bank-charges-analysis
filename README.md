
# Power BI Loan Interest Reconciliation Dashboard
A Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and reconcile loan interest and bank charges, helping identify variances and improve financial transaction monitoring.

📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI Loan Interest Reconciliation Dashboard developed to analyze loan transactions, reconcile interest payable against interest charged by the bank, and identify variances over the review period.

The dashboard transforms financial transaction data into meaningful insights that support loan reconciliation, interest verification, variance analysis, and financial review.

📸 Dashboard Preview




🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to provide a clear and interactive view of loan activity and interest calculations, enabling users to:

Reconcile interest payable against interest charged by the bank
Identify discrepancies and variances
Monitor loan disbursements and repayments
Reconstruct the outstanding loan balance
Compare contractual interest rates with applicable bank rates
Support financial review and reconciliation procedures

🔍 Dashboard Features
Interest Reconciliation

Comparison of reconstructed monthly interest payable against the interest charged by the bank.

Variance Analysis

Analysis of monthly variances to identify periods where differences occurred between expected and charged interest.

Loan Balance Reconstruction

Reconstruction and monitoring of the loan balance throughout the review period based on disbursements and repayments.

Interest Rate Analysis

Comparison of the contractual interest rate against the bank rate during the review period.

Interactive Filtering

Users can filter the analysis by:

Value Date
Transaction Description
Review period
💡 Key Insights

Based on the reconciliation performed:

The facility was initially disbursed at approximately ₦1.60 billion, with repayments substantially reducing the outstanding balance during the review period.
Total interest charged by the bank was approximately ₦286.45 million, compared with reconstructed interest payable of approximately ₦286.44 million.
The reconciliation resulted in an overall variance of approximately ₦9,018.35, representing an immaterial difference relative to the total interest payable.
Specific interest periods showed variances, although the overall cumulative difference was immaterial.
The reconstructed loan balance reduced significantly following substantial repayments, resulting in a final rebuilt balance of approximately ₦1.09 million.
The contractual interest rate was 18%, while the bank rate varied during certain periods of the review.

📋 Reconciliation Approach

The analysis involved:

Reviewing loan disbursement and repayment transactions.
Reconstructing the loan balance over the review period.
Calculating expected monthly interest payable.
Comparing reconstructed interest with interest charged by the bank.
Calculating monthly and cumulative variances.
Reviewing contractual and bank interest rates.
Presenting the results through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
